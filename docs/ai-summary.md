# AI Summary Layer

## Role in the System

The AI Summary layer is responsible for converting structured analytical outputs into concise, human-readable summaries. It operates strictly as a post-analysis augmentation layer and does not influence system decisions, alert thresholds, or execution logic.

This layer exists to improve interpretability and reduce cognitive load, not to introduce new signals or recommendations.

## Input Constraints & Preconditions

The AI Summary layer is invoked only after:
- Technical analysis has been completed successfully.
- Outputs are fully structured and validated.
- No additional data collection is required.

The AI receives only finalized analytical results and contextual metadata. Raw market data, execution logic, and system state are not exposed to this layer.

---

## Output Characteristics

Summaries generated by this layer:
- Reflect existing analytical outputs without reinterpretation.
- Avoid directional recommendations or predictive statements.
- Are explicitly non-authoritative and informational.

All summaries are treated as presentation artifacts rather than decision inputs.

---

## Risk Mitigation & Hallucination Control

To reduce the risk of misleading outputs:
- The AI layer is isolated from execution paths.
- Summaries are generated only from validated inputs.
- No autonomous decision-making authority is granted.
- Outputs are clearly distinguished from system-generated signals.

These constraints ensure that AI behavior cannot override deterministic logic.

---

## Failure Handling

If the AI summary process fails or produces unusable output:
- The system continues to function without interruption.
- Core alerts and analytical results remain available.
- The failure does not block execution or notification flows.

This design prevents AI dependency from becoming a system risk.

---

## Design Rationale

The AI Summary layer was intentionally scoped to:
- Enhance readability without introducing ambiguity.
- Preserve explainability of system behavior.
- Support optional augmentation rather than mandatory dependency.

This approach aligns with responsible AI usage in decision-support systems.

---

## Summary

The AI Summary layer adds interpretive convenience without altering system behavior. By enforcing strict boundaries and non-authoritative outputs, it improves user experience while maintaining trust, predictability, and control.
